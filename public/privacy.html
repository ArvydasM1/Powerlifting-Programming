5/3/1 Forever Log — privacy policy

This is a personal training log. It has no server and no account. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, the app can:

  • Write exercise sessions: the start and end time of each training session and one segment per set with its exercise type and repetition count. No weights are written.
  • Read heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep sessions and body weight, only for the time window around each training session and only to show them next to that session in the app.

Read data is stored on the device with the session it belongs to. It is never uploaded by the app. Sharing a session or an export includes health data only if you tick the "include health data" box for that share.

Bluetooth

If you connect a heart-rate band, live samples are recorded on the device for the open session only.

AI analysis

Analysis runs only when you tap Analyse and confirm what will be sent. Health data is excluded unless you tick the box. Keys you enter are stored in the device's secure storage and never exported.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. Sessions written to Health Connect can be removed from the Health Connect app or by deleting the session in this app.
